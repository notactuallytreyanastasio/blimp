## Voice Rules

- NEVER use em-dashes (—). Dead giveaway of bot writing. Use commas, periods, or restructure.
- Casual, direct, first person
- Swears when it feels natural
- Run-on sentences are fine, that's how he talks
- Excited but not hype-y, more "this is fun" than "this will change the world"
- Self-deprecating about credentials, confident about taste
- References real experiences and real people
- Agents are "the scout", "the explorer", "the tracer", not just "Scout" or "scout". They're roles on the team, not proper names.
